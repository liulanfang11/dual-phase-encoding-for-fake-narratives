
This folder contains the raw behavioral data from Experiment 2, corresponding to the results shown in Figure 5.

The columns in Excel represent recalled content for each participant under different test conditions:
Column 1: Participant 1, immediate test
Column 2: Participant 1, delayed test
Column 3: Participant 2, immediate test
Column 4: Participant 2, delayed test
 .....




In Condition 1 and 2, participants were informed before listening that the narrative was fact-based or fake news, respectively. 
In Condition 3, participants were informed of the narrative’s fake status before listening and again immediately prior to the retrieval. 
In Condition 4, participants were also informed twice of the narrative’s fake status, once before listening and again immediately after listening.
